## 1. Executive Summary

We are pleased to present **"BAUST Project Showcase,"** a comprehensive digital platform designed specifically for Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST). This application serves as a centralized hub for archiving, managing, and showcasing student innovations, research, and academic projects. We request approval to officially launch this platform for students and faculty.

## 2. Problem Statement & Solution

**Current Challenges:**
*   **Lack of Visibility:** Excellent student projects often go unnoticed after their initial presentation.
*   **Archiving Issues:** Physical reports and local files are hard to retrieve for future reference or accreditation purposes.
*   **Knowledge Silos:** Junior students lack easy access to previous work for inspiration or guidance.
*   **Limited Access:** Projects are constrained to physical labs or libraries.

**Our Solution:**
The **BAUST Project Showcase** app solves these problems by digitizing the entire project lifecycle. It creates a permanent, searchable, and globally accessible digital archive of BAUST's academic excellence.

## 3. Strategic Platform Deployment: Hybrid Approach

We have adopted a smart, hybrid distribution strategy to ensure 100% accessibility across all devices without incurring unnecessary costs.

*   **🤖 For Android Users (Native App):**
    *   The application is deployed as a **Native Flutter Application (APK)** to ensure maximum performance, fluid animations, and deep system integration for the majority of our student base using Android devices.

*   **🍎 For iOS Users (PWA Solution):**
    *   **The Challenge:** Publishing a native iOS app on the Apple App Store requires a mandatory developer fee of **$99 USD/year**, creating a recurring financial burden for academic projects.
    *   **The Solution:** We have built a specialized **Progressive Web App (PWA)** version specifically for iPhone/iPad users.
    *   **Experience:** It functions identically to a native app (supports full-screen usage, offline mode, and home screen installation) but is installed cost-free directly via Safari.
    *   **Hosting:** This version is hosted via **GitHub Pages**, ensuring free, unlimited, and permanent hosting.

**Impact:** This dual-strategy ensures that **every student and faculty member** has a premium mobile experience, regardless of their device, while keeping the project's operational cost at **Zero**.

## 4. Key Features

### 🔐 Secure Authentication & Accounts
*   **Google Sign-In Integration:** Utilizing university or personal Gmail accounts for secure, password-less entry.
*   **Role-Based Access:** Distinct dashboards and permissions for **Students**, **Teachers**, and **Admins**.
*   **🛡️ Teacher Account Verification:** To prevent unauthorized access, **Teacher accounts cannot be created freely.** Anyone signing up as a teacher is placed in a "Pending" state and must be explicitly **Approved by an Admin** (Department Head/Authority) before they can access the teacher dashboard or approve projects.
*   **🔑 Administrative Hierarchy:** Admins possess the authority to **promote other verified Teachers to Admin status**, ensuring effortless delegation and sustainable management of the platform over time.

### ✅ Controlled Approval Workflow
*   **No Direct Uploads:** Projects uploaded by students do **not** go live immediately. They are submitted to a specific queue.
*   **Supervisor Approval:** The project must be reviewed and **Approved** by the selected Supervisor/Teacher. This ensures that only verified, high-quality, and appropriate content is published to the public archive.

### 📱 User Experience & Accessibility
*   **Smart QR Sharing:** A built-in QR code sharing feature allows users to instantly share the app or specific projects with peers, enabling rapid adoption on campus.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS.
*   
*   **Offline Mode:** Core features remain accessible even without active internet.
